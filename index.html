Wine Selector
Privacy

The best pairing and the honest price, from the list in front of you.

Photograph the page of a restaurant's wine list and Wine Selector gives you one pick: the wine that suits your dish best and costs the least over its real retail price. Each wine gets one line: its markup over retail, whether that price is verified, the producer and the pairing.

A personal app, shared free with family and friends through TestFlight. For adults of legal drinking age.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: hello@wine-selector.app. In TestFlight you can also send feedback with a screenshot from inside the app.
